Task 3 – Data Visualization Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project is completed as part of Task 3 – Data Visualization Dashboard of my internship.

The objective of this task is to analyze the Superstore Sales Dataset and present meaningful insights using different data visualization techniques.


📊 Visualizations Created
1. Bar Chart – Sales by Category

Shows and compares the total sales for different product categories.

Insight: This visualization helps identify the category generating the highest sales.

2. Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend

Shows the change in sales over different months.

Insight: This visualization helps identify sales trends and periods of higher or lower sales.

3. Pie Chart – Sales by Customer Segment

Shows the percentage contribution of each customer segment:

Consumer
Corporate
Home Office

Insight: This helps identify which customer segment contributes the most to total sales.

4. Histogram – Profit Distribution

Shows how profit values are distributed across the dataset.

Insight: This helps identify the concentration of profits and loss-making transactions.

5. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Profit

Shows the relationship between sales and profit.

Insight: This helps identify patterns between sales and profit and detect unusual transactions or outliers.

🔍 Key Findings
Sales performance varies across different product categories.
Customer segments contribute different proportions of total sales.
Monthly sales show variations over time.
The profit distribution contains both profitable and loss-making transactions.
The Sales vs Profit visualization helps identify transactions with unusual sales or profit value.
🎯 Conclusion

The Superstore Sales Dataset was analyzed using Python and visualized through multiple chart types. The visualizations provide a clear understanding of sales performance, customer segments, sales trends, profit distribution, and the relationship between sales and profit.
